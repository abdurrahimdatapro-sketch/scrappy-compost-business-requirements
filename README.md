# Scrappy Compost – Business Requirements Analysis

## Project Overview

This project is a business analysis case study for Scrappy Compost, a small composting organization looking to expand its existing website to support online customer subscriptions.

The project focuses on analyzing business requirements, improving the customer acquisition process, and modeling how the new website will interact with the existing customer database and delivery process.

## Business Problem

Currently, Scrappy Compost customers can only sign up for or manage their subscriptions by phone or in person.

The proposed website expansion will allow customers to:

- Create an account
- Sign up for a composting subscription
- Enter their contact and payment information
- Update their customer information
- Start or cancel a subscription
- Manage their subscription online

The new website will also connect customer information with the existing customer database and delivery process.

## Project Goals

The project has the following goals:

- Increase subscribers by at least 20% by next spring
- Achieve an 8% click-through rate from Google AdWords advertising
- Enable customers to subscribe online
- Allow existing customers to manage their subscriptions online
- Connect online subscriptions with the existing customer database
- Update the delivery process when new customers subscribe

## Business Requirements

The website should allow customers to:

1. Create a username and account
2. Enter their name, email address, phone number, address, and credit card information
3. Subscribe to the composting service
4. Log in to their account
5. Update their information
6. Start or cancel their subscription

Customer information should be added to or updated in the existing customer database.

## Business Process

The proposed customer acquisition process can be summarized as:

**Website → Customer Information → Customer Database → Delivery Process**

The project also includes updates to the existing customer acquisition process flow and domain model.
